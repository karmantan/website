Lesson #32
Heroes are made
2026.08.29

Ichigo doesn't really have any grand goals compared to the others in the Big 3, like Luffy wanting to be King of the Pirates and Naruto wanting to be Hokage. Ichigo just sort of gets thrust into the whole Shinigami thing. Sure, we learn more about his history later on, and how he's actually a pretty unique being, for lack of better words. Ultimately though, Rukia stabbed him to give him her powers and he took on the Shinigami mantle. On top of that, he willingly powered up so he can protect his friends. He's just a really good kid. Orihime and Chad are also really good kids, who are kind of dragged into it. Uryu might be a bit different.
